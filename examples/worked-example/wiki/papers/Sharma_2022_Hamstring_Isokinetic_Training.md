# Mode-specific effects of concentric and eccentric isokinetic training of the hamstring muscle (Sharma 2022)

## Research Question

Are the effects of 6 weeks of isokinetic eccentric versus concentric hamstring training at 60°/s mode-specific, on the functional hamstring-to-quadriceps ratio (PT ecc/PT con), acceleration time, deceleration time and time to peak torque?

## Key Claims

- Both training modes raised eccentric hamstring peak torque and the functional ratio, and shortened hamstring and quadriceps acceleration time (within-group, Table 3).
- Between groups, only quadriceps time to peak torque differed (Table 4, p = 0.015); the authors read the effects on PT ecc, acceleration time and the ratio as not mode-specific.

## Method

- **Design**: two-arm pre-post randomized trial, participants and outcome assessor unaware of allocation; n = 30 healthy, recreationally active men, 15 per group.
- **Intervention**: non-dominant leg, Biodex Multi-Joint System 4, 60°/s, 3 sets of 10 repetitions, 2 days per week for 6 weeks.
- **Analysis**: Shapiro-Wilk, then Wilcoxon signed-rank (within) and Mann-Whitney U (between), Cohen's d.

## Evidence & Key Results

| Outcome | Ecc group, baseline to post | Conc group, baseline to post | Between-group post (Table 4) |
|---|---|---|---|
| PT ecc (N-m) | 119.77 ± 19.44 to 145.58 ± 31.60 | 115.34 ± 32.08 to 130.28 ± 27.15 | p = 0.254, d = 0.51 |
| PT ecc/PT con | 0.74 ± 0.09 to 0.87 ± 0.08 | 0.76 ± 0.11 to 0.83 ± 0.13 | p = 0.237, d = 0.35 |
| TPT quad (ms) | 447.33 ± 78.23 to 419.33 ± 85.06 | 494.67 ± 110.18 to 495.33 ± 98.11 | p = 0.015, d = 0.82 |

Within-group, PT ecc/PT con rose in both groups (ecc p = 0.002; conc p = 0.026, Table 3).

## Strengths

- Randomized, with blinded participants and outcome assessor; JATS tables checked cell by cell.

## Limitations
<!-- The wiki's own reading unless a bullet ends with "authors' own". -->

- Small sample (n = 15 per group) with post hoc power of 0.05 to 0.56 across the between-group tests (Table 4). Not stated by the authors as a limitation.
- Only male participants, only recreationally active, one angular velocity (60°/s) - *authors' own*.

## Relevance

A worked example of mode specificity for a hamstring protocol, useful as a counterpoint to quadriceps studies.
