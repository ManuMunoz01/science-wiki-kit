# Functional Hamstring-to-Quadriceps Ratio

## Definition
> [!abstract]
> The ratio of eccentric hamstring peak torque to concentric quadriceps peak torque (PT ecc/PT con), taken as a more functional index of knee muscle balance than the concentric-concentric ratio [[Sharma_2022_Hamstring_Isokinetic_Training]].

Single-source synthesis, corroborate when a 2nd source is ingested.

## Key Findings

- After 6 weeks of isokinetic hamstring training at 60°/s, the ratio rose from 0.74 ± 0.09 to 0.87 ± 0.08 with eccentric training and from 0.76 ± 0.11 to 0.83 ± 0.13 with concentric training (n = 15 per group); the groups did not differ after training (p = 0.237, d = 0.35) - [[Sharma_2022_Hamstring_Isokinetic_Training]]

## Controversies or Gaps
> [!warning]
> No second source yet: whether eccentric training raises the ratio more than concentric training is not settled by one trial of 15 per group.
