# Training Mode Specificity

## Definition
> [!abstract]
> Whether training with one contraction mode (eccentric or concentric) improves strength mainly in that mode, or in both [[Sharma_2022_Hamstring_Isokinetic_Training]].

Single-source synthesis, corroborate when a 2nd source is ingested.

## Key Findings

- In healthy men, concentric hamstring training at 60°/s raised eccentric peak torque (115.34 ± 32.08 to 130.28 ± 27.15 N-m, within-group p = 0.001), and the post-training difference from eccentric training was not significant (p = 0.254, d = 0.51); the authors conclude the effects on eccentric peak torque, acceleration time and the functional ratio are not mode-specific - [[Sharma_2022_Hamstring_Isokinetic_Training]]

## Controversies or Gaps
> [!warning]
> The authors themselves note conflicting earlier reports (some quadriceps studies found strongly mode-specific effects); those studies are not in this wiki yet, so the disagreement cannot be sourced here.
