# Field taxonomy (sport-science example)

Twelve fields, each with subfield slugs. Adapt the structure (field, subfield-slugs) to your own discipline and register it in `WIKI_SCHEMA.md`.

- Exercise Physiology (muscle-physiology, cardiovascular, metabolic, endocrine, thermoregulation, fatigue)
- Biomechanics (kinematics, kinetics, musculoskeletal-modeling, sport-specific-biomechanics)
- Strength & Conditioning (resistance-training, eccentric-training, plyometric, speed-agility, flexibility, periodization)
- Measurement & Assessment (dynamometry, validity-reliability, field-testing, body-composition, performance-monitoring)
- Sport Medicine & Injury (injury-epidemiology, shoulder, knee, hamstring, muscle-injury, rehabilitation-rtp, concussion)
- Sport Performance (handball, judo, football-soccer, athletics, swimming, racket-sports, combat-sports, team-sports-general)
- Motor Control & Learning (motor-learning, coordination-variability, balance-postural-control)
- Sport Psychology (motivation-sdt, anxiety-performance, psychological-skills, team-dynamics)
- Sport Nutrition (macronutrient-strategies, hydration, supplements-ergogenic, body-composition-management)
- Recovery & Adaptation (sleep-recovery, recovery-modalities, overtraining-overreaching, doms-muscle-damage)
- Applied Technology & Data Science (ml-sport, wearable-technology, video-analysis, data-analytics-sport)
- Research Methods & Statistics (study-design, systematic-review-meta-analysis, statistical-analysis, measurement-theory, scientific-writing, research-ethics)
